About us

Our expertise, put to work for a purpose.

Who we are

Khada N. Acharya is the founder, owner, and visual artist behind Kart Production — a student, writer, and photographer based in Essex, VT, with interests spanning photography, design, web development, and programming. Learn more at khadanacharya.carrd.co.

Our journey

Creative talent spanning from photography, graphic design, music, and video editing. Our portfolio ranges from artistic visual storytelling to multimedia campaigns built for impact — including Projects (2020, 20Twenty & Beyond, SAVE, and WAR), and Hope For A Change.

Diverse creativity

From striking visual design to stories worth telling, Kart Production spans a range of creative mediums — bringing versatility and an innovative flair to every audience it reaches.

Our creative vision

Our mission is to put creativity to work for positive change — building visual and auditory experiences that leave a lasting impression, deepen understanding of social issues, and spark real conversation.
